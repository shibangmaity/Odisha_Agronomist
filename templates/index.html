About the app

What is Odisha Agronomist?

Odisha Agronomist is a crop advisory app for farmers in Odisha. You describe your field — district, soil nutrients (N, P, K), soil pH and local weather — and it tells you which crops suit those conditions, what yields farmers in Odisha have typically seen, and a practical plan for sowing, fertilizer and irrigation, written in your own language.

What happens when you tap “Get advice”

1

You describe your field

Pick your district and enter nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, pH, temperature, humidity and rainfall. Optionally choose a crop you already have in mind.

2

The ML model scores 22 crops

A trained classifier compares your numbers with what each crop usually needs and gives every crop a suitability score. It reaches about 99% accuracy on test data it never saw.

3

Odisha's yield records are checked

For crops with local data, you see the typical yield range from historical Odisha records: your district if there is enough data, otherwise all of Odisha.

4

An AI agronomist writes your plan

A large language model turns your numbers, the chosen crop and the yield range into a sowing, fertilizer and irrigation plan in English, Odia, Hindi, Bengali or Telugu.

What you get

🎯

Top 3 crop matches

Ranked by suitability. Tap any one to get advice for that crop.

📏

Field-fit check

See which of your inputs sit inside, below or above the crop's usual range.

📊

Low, typical and high yield with the number of records behind it.

🗓️

When to sow, how to feed the soil and how to water, in your language.

Data and technology

Trained on the Crop Recommendation dataset (2,200 rows, 22 crops, 7 inputs). Yield ranges come from the Indian crop production dataset, filtered to Odisha (43 crops).

Good to know

  • Crop scores use soil and weather values only. Your district is used for the yield range and the AI's local advice.
  • The model learned from a general crop dataset, so treat scores as guidance, not a guarantee.
  • Some crops, like mango and coconut, have no Odisha yield records, so no yield range is shown for them.
  • Yield values in the source data mix units, so the app shows a range from the data instead of a prediction.
  • Always confirm fertilizer doses with your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) before buying inputs.
