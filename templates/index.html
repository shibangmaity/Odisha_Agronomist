What is Odisha Agronomist?

Odisha Agronomist is a crop advisory application for farmers in Odisha. It uses soil and weather values to recommend suitable crops and uses AI to provide cultivation, irrigation, fertilizer and crop-health guidance.

Available features

1

Crop recommendation

The trained ML model recommends suitable crops from soil and weather values.

2

AI cultivation advice

The AI provides sowing, fertilizer and irrigation suggestions for the selected crop.

3

Crop health advisor

Farmers can type a district, crop, disease/problem, symptoms and growth stage to receive crop-health guidance.

4

Area disease history

The AI explains general diseases and pests commonly associated with the crop and selected Odisha district.

🩺 Crop Health Advisor

The Crop Health Advisor provides a possible assessment, symptoms to check, treatment and management, medicine guidance, cultivation method, prevention and general disease-history information.

This is AI-generated guidance, not a confirmed diagnosis or verified year-by-year disease-surveillance record. Confirm chemical treatment with a local Krishi Vigyan Kendra, agriculture officer or plant pathologist.

Data and technology

The crop recommendation model uses soil and weather features. Yield information comes from the existing Odisha yield records. Crop-health responses are generated by an AI model and do not use a separate disease CSV or disease JSON database.
